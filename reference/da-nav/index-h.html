Value investing redefined

Harris Associates is a Chicago-based investment management firm. Since 1976 we have applied a single, consistent philosophy: buy businesses for less than we believe they are worth, and hold them until the market agrees.

Who we are

We are value investors. We estimate a company's intrinsic value from its cash flows and balance sheet, buy at a meaningful discount to that estimate, and give the investment the time it needs. That approach has not changed in five decades.

U.S., global and international equity strategies, delivered through separate accounts, commingled vehicles, sub-advisory relationships, mutual funds and ETFs.
